## What changed in 1.19.0

- Working-hours pay history: `payrollTimePolicyHistory` (append-only `{ effectiveFrom, policy }[]`) and `payrollTimePolicyRevision` on the organisation; `effectivePayrollTimePolicy` / `payrollTimePolicyForDayFromState` / `payrollTimePolicyTimeline` read history first (`payrollTimePolicyHistoryEntries`, `parsePayrollTimePolicyHistory`, `PAYROLL_POLICY_HISTORY_START`), falling back to prior / current / scheduled for a company with none. Apply now, promotion, Schedule and Cancel append to it (seeding it from the flat fields on the first write) and never remove an entry; a third change no longer re-prices days before the second (§1, §4.3).
- Two-admin guard: working-hours saves run in a transaction and are refused when the working hours changed since the screen loaded them (`payrollTimePolicySaveConflict`, `PAYROLL_POLICY_STALE_SAVE_MESSAGE`) (§4.3).
- Every consumer prices a day under the policy in effect on THAT day from the whole state: timesheets and invoices (incl. a change scheduled inside the pay run), weekly report pay rows, feed and day units, unbooked-labour warnings (`unbookedLabourRows` takes `payrollPolicy` as a state and reads each day), booking previews and timelines, Daily Overview and subcontractor edit sheets (prefill `customHoursPrefillForDays`), Home "Up next". Signed timesheets are never re-priced (§4.3, §5, §6, §7, §8).
- Weekend lunch breaks are fully per day: `saturday` / `sunday` `breakPaid` (missing = paid), `breakMinutes`, `breakWindowStart`, `breakWindowEnd`, each missing field the weekday one (`weekendBreakFor`, `payrollBreakOnDay`, `unpaidBreakAppliesOnDay`, `weekendLunchBreakCopy`, `payrollDayWindows`; weekday limits in `validatePayrollTimePolicy`); owner example: Saturday break 45 min 11:30–12:15 → 7.25h; `payrollHoursResult` takes it off by the weekday rule (set window and every-hour modes) and returns `unpaidBreakHours`; `customHoursBreakWarning` and the "No unpaid break" toggle on a weekend day only when its break is unpaid; `weekendBookingBreakdown` rows gain `unpaidBreakHours` and `lines`. Raise `minimumIosBuild` before any company switches a weekend break to Unpaid (§4.3, §8).
- Weekend unbooked labour (owner design): `warningDetection.weekendCoverage` — Saturday / Sunday toggles and "Hours to cover" windows under include-weekends (`parseWeekendCoverage`, `resolveWeekendCoverageDay`, `windowCoverage`; time inside that day's lunch-break window never needs covering); `unbookedLabourRows`, `unbookedLabourRowsOnDay` and `unbookedScanDays` use it instead of the weekend "counts as" hours; Daily Overview follows (§4.6, §5, §6).
- Working hours shows a live calculation under Saturday and Sunday, as Monday–Friday has (`weekendBookingBreakdown`) (§4.3).
- Booking flows warn about a double booking only when the times overlap (My Schedule self-booking fixed on web); a double-booked manager or admin may book anyway with the orange row (§5).

## iOS work for 1.19.0

**Before any company switches a weekend lunch break to Unpaid (or sets its own weekend break), raise `minimumIosBuild` (§18) to the first iOS build that ships 1.19.0.** Older builds do not read `saturday` / `sunday` `breakPaid`, `breakMinutes`, `breakWindowStart`, `breakWindowEnd` (they pay every weekend hour, so their timesheets and invoices disagree with web), their Working hours save rewrites the weekend day maps without them (an Unpaid choice turns back into Paid), and they do not read `warningDetection.weekendCoverage` (their unbooked-labour warnings still use the weekend "counts as" hours). Older builds also do not write `payrollTimePolicyHistory` / `payrollTimePolicyRevision`; the reader tolerates that (the flat current policy counts from `payrollTimePolicyEffectiveFrom`), but a change an older build promotes after the last history entry loses its own start day for the days before the older build's save.
