## What changed in 1.44.0

- **The Monday–Friday paid day is always derived** (§4.3, §8; owner decision 14 Nov, question A option 1): `weekdayPaidHours` is `derivedWeekdayPaidHours` (finish − start − the unpaid break, read into 1–12) for every policy, including the current, prior, scheduled and history policies saved before 1.20.0 without `paidHoursDerived`; the stored `standardPaidHours` is no longer read for pay (still written for older builds, prior and history written back as stored). `validatePayrollTimePolicy` checks the derived day for every policy. A weekend day with no "counts as" of its own reads 8h in the reader and the pay rule alike (`weekendCountsAsHours`, `DEFAULT_WEEKEND_COUNTS_AS_HOURS`; `payrollHoursResult`, `weekendBookingNote`, `weekendBookingBreakdown`, `weekendDayPreview`, `bookingHoursExplanation`, the setup wizard) — never the weekday paid day. Only unsigned timesheets change; signed snapshots and signed invoices are never re-priced, and a signed day-rate line reads from its stored days (web `signedLabourSlice`, the invoice's signed rows and the weekly report). Owner example 07:00–16:00, 30 min unpaid, booking 07:00–19:00 at ×2, £250 → £426.47.
- **No 20-minute half-day snap on weekdays for any policy** (§8): it was tied to `paidHoursDerived` (pre-1.20 policies only); custom weekday hours always pay their clock. Owner to confirm, as part of decision A = 1.
- Web: the 8-hour fallbacks on the paid-day division are gone where the rulebook value is always available — `resolvePayrollRate` / `resolveForTimesheetDay` drop their unused `standardDayHours`, `invoiceLinesForTimesheet` and `buildTimesheetInvoiceHtml` take it as required (the invoice HTML's rate-not-set fallback divided by 8 because the period page never passed it), the unused `estimatedDays` / `estimatedAmount` are deleted, `hoursBreakdown` and My Schedule's total drop their `|| 8`, and every timesheet line's `standardDayHours` is required (`timesheetHoursRateLine` no longer falls back to 8).
- **A booking is never hidden from a job's scheduling week** (§21, §11; owner decision 14 Nov, question H option 1, reverses the 1.41.0 "left off the grid"): `scheduleRowPerson` (an "Unknown person · 6A9632BA" row marked "Not linked to a person", no Quick Add) and Settings → Data health **Bookings not linked to a person** — `unresolvedBookingPeople`, `linkBookingPersonCandidates` (every users document, one per person, never the viewer: `LINK_BOOKING_PERSON_SELF_NOTE`), `linkBookingPersonRefusal` (fresh re-check in a transaction), `linkBookingPersonImpact` (what else the id brings: rate history, leave, timesheets, overtime, claims; a tick when pay may change), `linkBookingPersonConfirmMessage`, `linkBookingPersonDoneMessage` / `LINK_BOOKING_PERSON_ALREADY_MESSAGE`, `linkBookingPersonWriteFields` (an `arrayUnion` of the id onto the chosen person's `formerUserIds`). Web's grid now asks `resolveBookingPerson` through `scheduleRowPerson` (it kept its own `findUserByAnyId` lookup, which did not match `operatives` rows' accounts the same way).
- **One level for every roster shape** (§11, guardrail 1): `lineManagerCandidates` (and so `lineManagerSelectedIds`, `lineManagerSelectionAfterLevelChange`, `ghostCreateError`, the job manager picker's levels) reads a row whose `role` is the resolved `StaffAccountRole` object with `permissionLevelOf`'s rule — a flagless person is manager level, as on the stored document and in `lineManagerIds`. Flagless legacy managers now appear in every line-manager picker on both apps and pass the ghost save; operatives, pending invites and inactive people are unchanged. `priceWorkApproverCandidates` / `agreedManagerSuggestions` still require the stored manager flag or role (the security rules' approver test), so they do not change. Replaces web-only draft #320 (`lineManagerPickerRoster`).
- No security rules change (an admin already writes `formerUserIds` on a users document of their organisation, §16).
