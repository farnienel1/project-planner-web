# Canonical architecture

Web and iOS are separate repositories and separate languages. They share one executable business core. Screens, navigation, and platform persistence stay in each app.

## What is shared

The canonical module owns:

- Organisation id matching and which company a session should open (`chooseSessionOrganization`, `provisionalOrganizationId`, `orgAccessProbeFromReads`, `sessionMembershipIds`). Remembered keys: web `pp.webActiveOrg:{userId}`, iOS `cached_organizationId`. Same-user sign-out must not clear them (`shouldClearRememberedOrganization`). The switcher always keeps remembered / user-document / explicit companies even when a members query finished without them. Company name is `parseOrganizationName` / `organizationNameWriteFields` — a blank write is omitted so `name` cannot be wiped.
- The in-process organisation epoch used to drop a late result after a switch or sign-out
- Invoicing-period bounds, including wrapped payment runs and short months
- Organisation settings defaults and Firestore field names (`organizationSettings.ts`): payment runs write **both** `startDay`/`endDay` (web) and `startDate`/`endDate` (iOS); read prefers the iOS pair when both exist and disagree (`normalizePaymentRunRange`); empty ranges are 1–15 then 16–31; warningDetection is the top-level org map; payroll weekend hours use iOS field names; the material cut-off clock is `resolveMaterialCutOffClock` / `parseMaterialCutOff` on `settings.materialCutOff` (user-pref hour is never the clock)
- Who sees variations (`canSeeVariations`): every admin and manager, never an operative
- Warning coverage windows (`numberOfDays`, full Monday–Friday week, invoicing period)
- Whether a person covers the organisation standard day (`standardDayCoverage`). A full day (`FULL DAY` / `FULL_DAY`) covers 07:30–16:00 minus the unpaid break even when clock times are missing. Bookings match the person by user id or operative id. A shorter booking stays unbooked and the missing hours are the gap. Weekend days are included only when `warningDetection.includeWeekendsForUnbookedLabour` is on. That toggle and `warningDetection.excludedUserIdsFromUnbookedWarnings` apply to **both** the Warnings list and Daily Overview — Daily Overview must not keep a weekday-only unbooked gate.
- Organisation time zone for those windows (default `Europe/London`, never the device zone)
- Named booking slots (`FULL DAY` / `FULL_DAY`, `AM`, `PM`) and their pay (`paidHoursForNamedSlot`)
- The standard day and its two halves (`standardDayWindow`, `halfDayWindows`), and the clock interval any booking occupies (`slotInterval`). See "Standard day, AM and PM" below
- Whether two minute intervals clash, and interval merge / subtract (`intervalsOverlap`, `mergeMinuteIntervals`, `subtractMinuteIntervals`)
- Cache key shape `kind:organizationId`
- Which qualification warnings exist, which operatives are unverified, and which people are unbooked labour (`qualificationExpiryRows`, `unverifiedOperativeRows`, `unbookedLabourRows`). Daily Overview's "today" list is `unbookedLabourRowsOnDay` — the same rows for one day, not a second person loop. `includeWeekendsForUnbookedLabour` and `excludedUserIdsFromUnbookedWarnings` on `organizations/{orgId}.warningDetection` apply to Warnings **and** Daily Overview. An anonymised deleted profile (`isAnonymisedDeletedProfile`) is never a current warning, Daily Overview unbooked row, new weekly-report warning, or scheduling / book-labour candidate (`listedCurrentLabourPeople`). Historic bookings keep the stored name.
- Booking identity across Scheduling, Daily Overview, Warnings, and the weekly report (`warningRows.ts`):
  - `bookingOccupiesDay(status)`: Confirmed, Tentative (and the `pending` alias), and Completed put a person on a job that day; Cancelled does not. No screen keeps its own status list.
  - `labourBookingKind(person)`: `operative` (operative-mode account, booked in `bookings`), `manager` (manager / admin / super admin, booked in `managerSiteBookings`), or `null` (not labour). The scheduling picker offers exactly the people `unbookedLabourRows` can flag. A user with no role flags is not labour on either side.
  - `labourPersonIds` / `formerIdAliases`: a booking, leave row, or warning exclusion stored under an invite placeholder id still belongs to the person after sign-up moves them to `users/{authUid}`. `LabourPerson.formerIds` is read from `users/{uid}.formerUserIds`.
  - Daily Overview never hides a booking because its person cannot be named; it lists the row as "Unknown person".
- Display order of already-computed warning rows (`sortWarningsByDate`, `parseWarningsSortDirection`, `compareWarningDayKeys`). The sort key is the row's `dayKey` (the calendar/coverage day the warning is about — expiry day for qualifications, the booked/unbooked/leave/clash/material day for the others). Default is `oldest`. A blank or invalid `dayKey` sorts last either way. This does not hide rows or change detection. The Oldest/Newest choice is local to each device and signed-in user (web `localStorage` key `warningsSortDirection:{userId}`; iOS UserDefaults keyed by user id). Load it before the Warnings list paints; persist immediately on toggle; default `oldest` only when that user has never chosen on this device — never silently fall back after they have. It is not an organisation setting and must not be written to `warningDetection` or any company document.
- Which annual-leave warnings exist: a booking inside approved leave, and a half day whose other half is not booked (`leaveCoverageRows`)
- The key a dismissed expired-qualification warning is stored under (`qualificationDismissKey`, `withoutDismissedQualificationRows`)
- Who sees every job and every warning, who may add or edit a work catalogue, who may open User Timesheets, and which managers receive a job notification (`seesEveryJob`, `canViewStaffWarnings`, `canEditWorkCatalogue`, `canReviewUserTimesheets`, `seesEveryUserTimesheet`, `receivesJobNotification`)
- Active / Inactive / Pending on Manage Users, Managers, and Operatives (`rosterSegment`, `matchesRosterSegment`, `listedRosterUsers`, `isAnonymisedDeletedProfile`). Empty email plus the rosterAnonymisePatch name (`Deleted user`, or firstName `Deleted` + surname `user`) is Inactive even when `passwordSet` and `isActive` are still true. A pending invite whose email is still claimed at `organizations/{orgId}/userEmails/{email}` stays Pending. A leftover that still carries the deleted person's email, real name, or phone after that claim is gone is not listed. `claimedEmails` is the set of `userEmails` document ids from a successful read; null means that read failed, so a pending invite with an email is kept. “Deleted user” appears only under Inactive. Do not drop an empty-email tombstone when `users/{uid}.organizationId` still names this company — membership, `userEmails`, and `operativeProfiles` may already be gone. Re-invite of the same email is a new Pending row (`resolveInviteEmailClaim`); the tombstone stays Inactive. Block only a live `organizations/{orgId}/userEmails/{email}` claim pointing at a non-anonymised user who still holds that email, or a `users` doc in this org with that email that is a real member (`passwordSet` true) or a still-claimed pending invite. An anonymised tombstone, an identifiable leftover UUID placeholder (`isIdentifiablePendingLeftover`), and Auth-only leftovers do not block. Reuse or replace the leftover placeholder; never flip the tombstone back to a person. In-org Add User looks up that email only with `inviteEmailOrgLookupConstraints` (this org + this email) — never a collection-wide `users` query or Auth enumeration. The placeholder write is `invitePlaceholderUserWriteFields` (`passwordSet` false); `userCreateRequiresAgeConfirmation` is true only when the creator uid equals the users doc id. Admin invite Path 2/4 must not write `ageConfirmed` / `At` / `Years`. Day/hourly rates on invite go on `users/{uid}` (`payBasis`, `dayRate`, `hourlyRate`); `inviteWritesOperativeProfile` is false — do not write `organizations/{orgId}/operativeProfiles/{newUserId}` at Add User. That collection is a later fallback when a manager cannot write the user document. Do not invent a second passwordSet switch in a screen.
- The `users/{uid}` fields both apps must keep in sync, when PAYE ↔ self-employed takes effect, and the Edit User permission list (`USER_DOCUMENT_FIELDS`, `employmentTypeOnDay`, `applyEmploymentTypeChange`, `MANAGER_PERMISSION_TOGGLES`). Self-create age fields are `USER_SELF_CREATE_AGE_FIELDS`; they are not in `USER_DOCUMENT_FIELDS`. `userCreateRequiresAgeConfirmation` is true only when the creator uid equals the users doc id.
- User-level notification toggles, inbox destination, device-push allow-list, and unread badge (`parseUserNotificationPreferences`, `userNotificationPreferenceFieldPatch`, `notificationPreferenceAllowsDelivery`, `notificationPreferenceCopy`, `notificationPreferenceIsVisible`, `visibleNotificationPreferenceKeys`, `notificationDestination`, `devicePushNotificationTypes`, `notificationDeliversDevicePush`, `unreadNotificationBadgeCount`, `NOTIFICATION_ICON_BADGE_CLEARS_ON_FOREGROUND`). Keys live on `users/{uid}.notificationPreferences`. Which My notifications rows appear is `visibleNotificationPreferenceKeys` from role + Projects/Small works toggles + people/clients/materials flags (`NotificationPreferenceAudience`). Operatives do not see staff rows (new project / small works, booking clashes, new material order, new operative / manager / client, annual leave requested, line-manager peer updates, warning dismissed, timesheet sign-off). A manager sees New project / New small works only when `canEditWorkCatalogue` is true for that catalogue. Labels and descriptions are `notificationPreferenceCopy` — annual leave approved or declined is always “When your annual leave is approved or declined.” Hiding a toggle does not change fan-out. A save uses dotted paths and must not replace the map (legacy `bookingConflicts` and cut-off clock fields stay). A toggle that is on does not add recipients — fan-out stays `receivesJobNotification`. The centre lists only `inboxNotificationVisibleToUser` rows (stored `userId` matches, or a leftover row with no owner). Web queries `organizations/{orgId}/notifications` where `userId` is the signed-in user so a busy company does not download every member’s inbox. Newest `createdAt` first (`compareInboxNotificationsByCreatedAt`). Each app maps the returned `kind` to its own route; do not keep a second toggle list, deep-link table, or unread counter in a screen. Booking: admin → Daily Overview on that day; manager → that job’s scheduling page when `relatedId` + `catalogue` are present; operative → My Schedule. Missing id falls back to the section hub or Daily Overview. Unknown type → notifications. A closed iPhone is reached by one FCM send on create of the inbox row when `notificationDeliversDevicePush` and `notificationPreferenceAllowsDelivery` both allow it. The same document id must not push again on read, update, or next app open. APNs badge and the in-app red number are `unreadNotificationBadgeCount` for that `userId` on the same rows the centre lists. Opening the iPhone app clears the **icon** badge (`NOTIFICATION_ICON_BADGE_CLEARS_ON_FOREGROUND`); the in-app number stays until rows are read. On-device clocks stay local with no FCM: `material_order_cut_off`, `qualification_expiry`, `deadline_reminder`, `deadline_due`. Timesheet submission reminder is included once when its inbox row is first created at notify-at (`timesheetSubmissionNoticeDedupKey`); that write is the device alert — iOS must not also schedule a second local alert for the same id.
- Whether a person has a paid allowance or only a year-count, the leave-year window, remaining days, and a one-year remaining override (`hasAnnualLeaveAllowance`, `leaveYearBounds`, `annualLeaveBalance`, `applyRemainingOverride`)
- Material catalogue search (`tokenizeMaterialSearch`, `materialSearchScore`, `rankMaterialRecords`). Every typed token must match name, brand, code, category, size or length as an exact, prefix, or contained piece, so `2.5mm LS` finds `2.5mm2 Twin & Earth … LSZH`. Empty query keeps the original order.
- Qualification library search and section chips (`tokenizeQualificationSearch`, `qualificationSearchScore`, `rankQualificationRecords`, `qualificationLibraryFilterChips`, `qualificationMatchesSection`). Every typed token must match name, code, awarding body, section, subsection or notes. Section chips come from the library `section` field (`QUALIFICATION_LIBRARY_SECTIONS`); All shows every row; Other is rows with no section. Search still runs when a section chip is on. Empty query keeps the original order.
- Assigned qualifications on an operative (`assignedQualificationWriteFields`, `mergeOperativeQualificationWrite`, `mergeQualificationCertificateUrls`, `qualificationCertificateStoragePath`). The assignment write is `id`, `name`, `hasEndDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, optional `endDate` — library extras stay off this map. Certificate upload is after the assignment write and must not block it. On success merge `qualificationCertificateURLs[id] = downloadURL`. Never delete-all + rewrite; never drop other quals. Storage path is `organizations/{orgId}/operatives/{operativeId}/qualifications/{qualificationId}/certificates/{uid}_{ts}_{name}`.
- Material order required delivery (`defaultMaterialDeliveryRequiredAt`, `defaultMaterialDeliveryDayKey`, `materialOrderIncludesDeliveryRequired`, `formatMaterialDeliveryRequired`). The default is tomorrow 08:00 in the organisation zone when Send is opened on a weekday; Saturday or Sunday opens on the next Monday 08:00. The user can change the day and time. Quotes never include a required day or time — the quote template asks the wholesaler to confirm availability and time. The materials-list day (`materialsDate`) is not the delivery day. Orders write optional `deliveryRequiredAt` on `organizations/{orgId}/materialSendRecords` and keep every existing send-record field. Do not invent a second Monday/8am calculator in a screen.
- Timesheet submit deadline and reminder (`defaultTimesheetSubmissionFromPaymentDay`, `defaultTimesheetSubmissionFromPaymentWeekday`, `timesheetSubmissionNoticeCopy`, `dueTimesheetSubmissionNotices`). Deadline is close of play (17:00) the calendar day before the payment day; notify-at is 08:00 three calendar days before. Recurring uses the same offsets on weekdays. Copy is generated from the deadline picker and payment day. Do not invent a second 17th/19th or Monday/Friday calculator in a screen.
- Timesheet period identity, list status, and sign-off / export write shape (`timesheetPeriod.ts`: `timesheetDocumentId`, `timesheetPeriodFromInvoicing`, `timesheetListBucket`, `parseTimesheetStatusFields`, `timesheetSignOffWriteFields`, `timesheetExportWriteFields`, `selectableTimesheetPaymentRunPeriods`). A period is organisation + user + start/end day keys from the invoicing window, not an ISO week. Document id is `timesheet_{userId}_{unixStartOfDay(start)}` in the organisation zone. Awaiting = operative signed, line manager required, manager not signed. Signed off = operative signed and (no line manager, or `managerSignedAt` / `approvedAt`). Exported = `exportedAt` (Generate Invoice must not set it). Both apps write `managerSignedAt` and the `approvedAt` alias. Historical payment-run windows stay selectable after a mode/range change (`invoicing.paymentRunHistory` + stored `weekEndKey`). Who may open User Timesheets is `canReviewUserTimesheets`; who sees every person's row is `seesEveryUserTimesheet` (admins). Managers still filter to their reports in the app. Hours come from bookings in that stored window — do not hide a signed row behind `hours > 0` or remap a 1–7 sheet onto a live 1–15 window.
- Organisation subscription access (`hasAccess` in `billingAccess.ts`). Input is the nested `organizations/{orgId}.billing` map (Stripe field names unchanged: `status`, `lastPaymentFailedAt`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd`, `currentPeriodEnd`, `billingInterval`). Missing billing, `none`, `pending`, `trialing`, and `active` stay open. `past_due` stays open within 7 days of `lastPaymentFailedAt` (and when that timestamp is missing). `canceled`, `unpaid`, `paused`, `incomplete`, and expired `past_due` stay closed. Cancel-at-period-end stays open while status is still `active` — Stripe flips it to `canceled` when the period ends. Monthly and annual use the same rule. Do not invent a second status enum. iOS copy “subscription has run out” for every role when this returns false. The website banner stays cosmetic until a separate write-lock.

## Staff visibility, catalogue toggles, and notification recipients

Each app resolves four flags for the signed-in account (super admin, admin, manager, operative mode) with its own user store and passes them to `staffAccess.ts`. On the web that is `staffAccountRole` in `lib/permissions.ts`; on iOS it is `UserStore`.

- Lists, warnings, and variations. Every admin and every manager sees every project, every small works job, every warning, and every variation in the company, including jobs and people they are not the project manager or line manager for. They can open those warnings and fix them. Neither list is narrowed to assigned jobs. On the web `visibleWorks` in `lib/access/workAccess.ts` returns the whole catalogue for staff; a manager still does not see a job an admin hid from them on the job's View tab (`hiddenManagerUserIds`). Operatives see only the jobs they are booked onto and never get the staff warning list or variations. An account with no staff role keeps the assigned-or-booked rule. `canSeeVariations` is the variation gate; tracker reorder stays admin-only (`canManageVariationTracker`).
- Projects and Small works toggles. The two toggles never hide a list. With a toggle off, an admin or manager still sees every job in that catalogue but cannot add or edit it: create and edit affordances are hidden or disabled (`canEditWorkCatalogue`, web `canManageWorkCatalogue`, and the `ProjectForm` gate for deep links). Super admin ignores both toggles.
- Notifications stay narrower. A manager receives a notification for a job or a person only as an assigned project manager of that job or as that person's line manager (`receivesJobNotification`). Admins and super admins receive it. Seeing the job in the list does not make a manager a recipient, so widening the lists must not widen fan-out. On the web the recipient rows are written with `userId` set (`lib/firebase/notifyInbox.ts`, `lib/variations/variationStorage.ts`, `lib/timesheets/timesheetNotifications.ts`), and `lib/stores/notificationStore.ts` shows a targeted row only to that user.

## Organisation settings document

`organizations/{orgId}` is the company settings record. Both apps read and write the same maps (`ORGANIZATION_DOCUMENT_FIELDS`). A save on one device must not drop fields the other still uses.

- The company name is `organizations/{orgId}.name` (`parseOrganizationName` / `organizationNameToFirestore` / `organizationNameWriteFields`). Both apps write that field. Read prefers `name`, then leftover aliases (`organizationName`, `organisationName`, `companyName`, or the same keys under `settings`) so a document that only has an older key still displays. A save writes `name` only when it has a value — `organizationNameToFirestore('')` is `{}` so a stub or the other company cannot persist empty over a known name. Do not invent a second company-name field. Empty string is missing. Display uses `coalesceOrganizationName` then `displayOrganizationName`; a hydrated name must not be replaced by a stub `''`.
- Payment runs live on `invoicing.paymentRunDateRanges`. Each row writes **both** `startDay`/`endDay` and `startDate`/`endDate` with the same numbers. Read prefers `startDate`/`endDate` (iOS) then `startDay`/`endDay` (web), via `normalizePaymentRunRange`. A leftover web 1–16 cannot override an iOS-saved 1–15. A web-only 1–16 save (no `startDate`) is kept. Missing or empty ranges are 1–15 then 16–31 (`CANONICAL_HALF_MONTH_RANGES`). `invoicingPeriod` / `coverageWindow` parse those same field names — do not pass raw Firestore rows around the normalizer. That split is what warnings use when the scan mode is end of invoicing period. `parseInvoicing` keeps `paymentRunMode: date_ranges` unless the document is exactly `recurring_timeframe`. Date-range firms always get `paymentDateMode: specific_dates` — a missing or leftover `recurring_date` must not flip the pay-day UI (or a later save) to weekly. The 1–31 days-covered strip (`paymentRunShowsMonthCoverage`) is date-range mode only. Recurring weekday pay appears only when `paymentRunMode` is actually `recurring_timeframe`. An invoicing save merges into the existing `invoicing` map so unknown iOS keys survive. When the live window or mode changes, `paymentRunHistoryAfterChange` appends the previous window to `invoicing.paymentRunHistory` (same `startDay`/`endDay` + `startDate`/`endDate` pairs, plus recurring weekdays). Timesheet pickers call `selectableTimesheetPaymentRunPeriods` so a 1–7 run stays selectable after payroll becomes 1–15 or 1–5.
- Timesheet submission reminders live on `invoicing.timesheetSubmission` (one map per date-range payment date, or one map for a recurring run). Each map is `timesheetDeadlineDay`, `timesheetDeadlineWeekday`, `timesheetDeadlineHour`, `timesheetDeadlineMinute`, `notifyDay`, `notifyWeekday`, `notifyHour`, `notifyMinute`, `notifyCustomised`. Date-range rows also mirror those keys onto `paymentRunDateRanges[i]`. Recurring also writes the weekday/clock keys on the `invoicing` root. Defaults come from `defaultTimesheetSubmissionFromPaymentDay` / `defaultTimesheetSubmissionFromPaymentWeekday`: close of play (17:00) the calendar day before payday, and 08:00 three calendar days before (20 → 17th 08:00 / COP 19th; 5 → 2nd 08:00 / COP 4th; Friday → Tuesday 08:00 / Thursday COP). Payment days wrap the next month (16–31 paid on the 5th → notify 2nd / deadline 4th). Short months clamp. Changing the payment day refreshes defaults only when `notifyCustomised` is false. Copy is `timesheetSubmissionNoticeCopy`. Due occurrences are `dueTimesheetSubmissionNotices` in `Europe/London`. Web writes `organizations/{orgId}/notifications` with `type` `timesheet_submission_reminder` when the dashboard opens after notify-at. That first create is the device alert (`notificationDeliversDevicePush`); the Cloud Function sends one FCM push. Dedup key is `timesheetSubmissionNoticeDedupKey` (includes organisation id + run index + payment day + user id). iOS must show the same inbox row and must **not** schedule a second local alert for that id (unlike material cut-off, qualification expiry, and deadline reminder / due, which stay on-device clocks with no FCM). A save must not drop `noteToUsers`, payment-run pairs, `warningDetection`, or other invoicing keys.
- Warning detection is `organizations/{orgId}.warningDetection` (top-level, not `settings.warningDetection`). Default scan is `numberOfDays` = 7. The nested settings map may only fill excluded user ids when the top-level map left that key out. `includeWeekendsForUnbookedLabour` and `excludedUserIdsFromUnbookedWarnings` are the inputs to `unbookedLabourRows` / `unbookedLabourRowsOnDay`. Both the Warnings list (and its count) and Daily Overview's unbooked people for the viewed day — including Saturday/Sunday when the toggle is on — come from that function. Excluded `users/{uid}` ids never appear as unbooked on either screen.
- Payroll, annual-leave defaults, schedule options, and material cut-off parse/write through the same module. The cut-off clock is only `organizations/{orgId}.settings.materialCutOff` (`materialCutOffHour`, `materialCutOffMinute`, `materialCutOffOnSaturday`, `materialCutOffOnSunday`, `materialOrderCutOff`) via `parseMaterialCutOff` / `resolveMaterialCutOffClock`. A missing hour defaults to 16; a saved 15 and 30 stay 15:30. `users/{uid}.notificationPreferences.materialCutOffHour` is a leftover dual-write — never the clock once the organisation map is present, and never a fallback that restores 16:00 over a saved 15:30. Dual-writing the saver's user prefs on save must not become the source of truth. Cut-off stays an on-device clock (`daily-material-order-cutoff`); no FCM from an inbox mirror and no Cloud Function cancel path. Do not invent a second 16:00 default or a second clock calculator in a screen.
- Operational collection paths (`OPERATIONAL_COLLECTION_PATHS`) stay iOS-compatible: variations collection plus settings fallback, H&S under `settings/healthSafety_…`, dismissed warnings, bookings, timesheets, tasks, site audits, clients, catalogues, `materialSendRecords`. A material order may write `deliveryRequiredAt` on that send record; a quote must not. Keep every existing send-record field (`id`, `projectId`, `requestType`, `sentAt`, `materialsDate`, `sentBy`, `recipients`, `lines`). Staff who can open Send (not operatives) may create a send record when they belong to the company — membership is enough; a stale `users/{uid}.organizationId` must not fail the write after the email has already gone to the wholesaler.

## User profile document

Every account is `users/{uid}`. Both apps read and write the same fields (`USER_DOCUMENT_FIELDS`). A save on one device must not drop fields the other device still uses.

- `formerUserIds` (`USER_FORMER_IDS_FIELD`, `parseFormerUserIds`): invite placeholder ids this account used before sign-up. Accepting an invite writes it with `inviteMigrationFormerIdWriteFields` (web `completeInviteSetup`, `mergePlaceholderUserDocOntoAuthUidIfNeeded`; iOS `mergePlaceholderUserDocOntoAuthUidIfNeeded`). Never remove ids from it. Accounts that signed up before this field existed are repaired once with `scripts/backfill-former-user-ids.ts` (dry run proposes matches from invite timing; `--write` appends only reviewed entries).
- Account kind is `accountKindFromFlags`: super admin or admin access → admin; `operativeMode` → operative; otherwise manager.
- Employment type is `paye` or `self_employed` (`selfEmployed` on read is `self_employed`). Changing type asks which working day it starts. Immediate (or a date on/before today) writes the new type and clears the transition. A future date writes `employmentType` as the new type, `employmentTypeTransitionFrom` as the old type, and `employmentTypeEffectiveAt` as that day. `employmentTypeOnDay` is what timesheets and the weekly report use.
- Permission toggles on Edit User use `OPERATIVE_PERMISSION_TOGGLES` / `MANAGER_PERMISSION_TOGGLES` (order and copy). The flags themselves live on `users/{uid}.permissions`.
- Opening an operative from warnings or the roster is the same Edit User page (`/dashboard/users/{id}/edit?from=operatives`), not a second catalogue-only editor. Managers see every warning, but **Open operative** is hidden when their Operatives toggle is off — they cannot edit that profile. Admins who can manage users still see the button.
- `annualLeaveEnabled` is the paid-allowance toggle, not access to Holiday. Off (typical for self-employed staff without a set entitlement) still books leave. They see days taken in the company leave year (`annualLeaveBalance`); the count resets on the first day of the next year. On shows days per year and remaining. A mid-year joiner is set with `applyRemainingOverride` → `annualLeaveYearAllowance` + `annualLeaveYearAllowanceKey`. That remaining is for this leave year only and returns to Days per year after the year ends.
- `notificationPreferences` is the user-level My notifications map. Keys (all default on): `materialOrderCutOff`, `annualLeaveRequested`, `annualLeaveDecision`, `projectCreated`, `smallWorksCreated`, `bookingClashes` (read `bookingConflicts` when the new key is absent), `materialOrderPlaced`, `bookedOntoJob`, `operativeCreated`, `managerCreated`, `clientCreated`, `warningDismissed`, `tasksAssignedOrCompleted`, `toolboxTalkIssued`, `timesheetSignOff`, `qualificationExpiry`, `lineManagerPeerUpdates`, `timesheetSubmissionReminder`. Both apps render only `visibleNotificationPreferenceKeys` for that account and copy from `notificationPreferenceCopy`. Hidden keys stay on the map and keep their stored value. Cut-off clock fields (`materialCutOffHour`, `materialCutOffMinute`, `materialCutOffOnSaturday`, `materialCutOffOnSunday`) may still sit on the same map as an iOS dual-write of the company time — they are not this toggle list and they are not the clock. The organisation `settings.materialCutOff` map is. Inbox rows also accept optional `dayKey` and `catalogue` (`projects` | `smallWorks`) so a booking tap can open Daily Overview or that job’s scheduling page. `pushTokens` / `pushTokenUpdatedAt` stay on the user document; a profile save must not delete them. The onCreate Cloud Function reads those fields and `notificationPreferences` and must not drop them. After send it removes only tokens whose FCM result is invalid APNs credential, unregistered, or invalid registration token — never the whole array because one send failed. Missing token or a preference that is off → send nothing.

New organisations created on the web write the starter material catalogue into `organizations/{orgId}/materialCatalogue` and the starter qualification library into `organizations/{orgId}/qualifications`. The material catalogue still seeds only when that collection is empty (`starterCollectionShouldSeed`, true only for count 0). The qualification library merges any missing starter codes (`missingStarterQualificationCodes`) so a guided-setup custom row cannot block the 272. The web also merges missing library codes when Organisation Qualifications or Edit User loads. Never delete-all + rewrite. iOS reads those collections; it does not import the starter files again. Qualification templates always write `name`, `hasEndDate` (false on the library), `createdAt`, and `updatedAt`. Library rows also write `code` (document id), `section`, `subsection`, `awardingBody`, `level`, `renewYears`, `renewalType`, `status`, and `notes`. Assigned qualifications on an operative stay the existing iOS maps (`id`, `name`, `hasEndDate`, timestamps, optional `endDate`) plus `qualificationExpiryDates` and `qualificationCertificateURLs`. Merge those maps with `mergeOperativeQualificationWrite` so a new assignment cannot wipe other quals, and merge a certificate URL only after the assignment is stored. Merge missing library codes before restoring names from assignments.

## What stays in each app

- SwiftUI and React screens, navigation, and copy
- The Warnings Oldest/Newest preference (web `localStorage` `warningsSortDirection:{userId}`; iOS UserDefaults keyed by user id on that device). Independent per platform and per device. Not Firestore. Once a user chooses, that value must survive refresh, leaving Warnings, and sign-out / sign-in as the same user on that device.
- Firestore listeners, offline outbox, and local databases
- Clash timeline math and the material cut-off message (`lib/warnings/generateOrgWarnings.ts` and `Core/WarningsComputation.swift`). Those dates use the London business calendar on iOS. A full-day slot with no clock times uses the organisation standard day for overlap (`07:30`–`16:00` by default), the same window as iOS `ManagerScheduleInterval` and `OperativeBookingInterval`. Two of those on one person and day are a clash. The unbooked-labour exclusion list does not hide that clash. AM and PM still do not overlap.
- Overtime and break payroll (`Core/PayrollHoursEngine.swift` and the web timesheet helpers that are not named slots)

Do not add a second coverage window, a second unbooked-person loop, a second `FULL DAY` hour value, or a second AM/PM midpoint. Daily Overview must not invent a weekday-only unbooked list; it calls `unbookedLabourRows` / `unbookedLabourRowsOnDay` with the same `warningDetection` fields as Warnings.

## Standard day, AM and PM

One rule decides where the morning ends and the afternoon starts. Clash detection, annual-leave cover, the calendar export, Home "up next", Quick Add, and the iOS clash and leave scans all call `halfDayWindows(payrollTimePolicy)`.

1. The standard day is `[standardDayStart, standardDayEnd)`. An unparsable or inverted pair falls back to 07:30–16:00, so no setting can produce an empty or negative half. A break field the organisation never set falls back to 12:00–12:30 (`standardBreakWindow`); that is the unpaid break `standardDayCoverage` subtracts for unbooked labour, whether or not it also splits the day.
2. If the break window is valid, strictly inside the day, and leaves at least 60 minutes on each side, it is the split: AM = `[dayStart, breakStart)`, PM = `[breakEnd, dayEnd)`. The break belongs to neither half. Default company: AM 07:30–12:00, PM 12:30–16:00. 07:00–17:00 with a 12:30–13:30 break: AM 07:00–12:30, PM 13:30–17:00.
3. Otherwise the day splits at its wall-clock midpoint, floored to the minute. A company on 13:00–19:00 that left the break at 12:00–12:30 gets AM 13:00–16:00, PM 16:00–19:00. A break jammed against one edge (08:00–08:30) is ignored for the split for the same reason.
4. FULL DAY is the whole standard day. Custom hours are the clock times stored on the booking. Evening is the four hours after the day; overtime the two hours after that.
5. Pay is unchanged: FULL DAY pays `standardPaidHours`, AM and PM each pay half. The windows above decide clashes and cover, not money.

Because AM and PM are disjoint by construction, an AM booking never clashes with PM leave, and a PM booking never clashes with AM leave.

## Annual leave against bookings

`leaveCoverageRows` runs over the same coverage window as the other warnings and only looks at approved leave. Leave is FULL DAY, AM, or PM; anything else stored on a holiday document is treated as FULL DAY.

- **Booked during annual leave** (high). Any booking interval that overlaps the leave window. Full-day leave overlaps every booking that day. The row lists each booking and the overlapping minutes.
- **Half-day leave not covered** (medium). For AM or PM leave, the other half is the working half. Every booking interval that day is subtracted from it. Whatever is left is reported with clock ranges and hours. PM leave with a 07:30–09:30 custom booking on the default company reports "09:30–12:00 (2.5 hours) is not booked". No booking at all reports the whole half. The row respects `excludedUserIdsFromUnbookedWarnings` and `includeWeekendsForUnbookedLabour`; the clash row does not.

`unbookedLabourRows` already skips a person-day with any approved leave, so a half day produces exactly one warning: the cover row from this rule.

People are matched by account first (`userId`), then by any operative profile sharing the account's email. A roster operative without an account is matched by operative id.

## Dismissed warnings

An expired-qualification warning can be dismissed. The dismissal is `organizations/{orgId}/dismissedWarnings/{dismissKey}` where `dismissKey = qualificationDismissKey(operativeId, qualificationId, expiryDayKey)`. The key includes the expiry day, so saving a renewed certificate with a new date produces a fresh warning. Upcoming (not yet expired) rows are never hidden by a dismissal. Both apps read the collection and filter with `withoutDismissedQualificationRows`.

## Organisation context

One signed-in session has one current organisation id.

Web and iOS both call `chooseSessionOrganization` (and `provisionalOrganizationId` before probes finish). Order: explicit switch, then the company **this device** remembered, then `users/{uid}.organizationId`. A confirmed non-member is skipped. A slow, failed, missing, permission-denied, or unconfirmed first-tick / cache-only membership read is `unknown` (`orgAccessProbeFromReads` with `unconfirmed`) and must not throw away the remembered company. Do not invent a second picker that prefers the user document or an incomplete membership list.

Web remembers `pp.webActiveOrg:{userId}` (`webSessionOrganizationStorageKey`). iOS already remembers `cached_organizationId` — do not invent a second phone key. Switching writes that device key. Login persist is `persistOrganizationId` from the chooser: the remembered company when it wins; **null** when falling back to the user-document company — including when this device has no remembered key yet — so a remount that missed local storage or treated a cache read as denied cannot overwrite this device. Web must not write `users/{uid}.organizationId` on switch or login. iOS may keep writing that field on an explicit switch (existing `switchActiveOrganization` write) but must not copy it into `cached_organizationId` on restore. A restore / token refresh / new-bundle remount that opens the user-document company must leave the device key as it is.

The browser and the phone may show different companies. Each device has one active company. Same-user sign-out must keep the remembered key (`rememberedOrganizationSurvivesSignOut` / `shouldClearRememberedOrganization`). Clear only when a **different** account is about to use the same slot.

The switcher lists every company the user is a member of (`members.{uid}`, `creatorUserId`, `users/{uid}/orgMemberships`). That list is not the session chooser. `sessionMembershipIds` **always** unions discovered ids with remembered, user-document, explicit, and the last list — including when the members/creator query “finished” but omitted a company this device already knows. A finished query that dropped the other company is still a miss. Known company documents paint first (`onKnown`); the 12s members/creator scan must not hide them or delay the first list. “Logged into the other one” must not hide the missing company from the list.

## Switching organisation

Switching is not a single assignment.

1. Membership in the destination company is checked before its data is shown.
2. `adoptCurrentOrganization` advances the epoch on web. iOS replaces `organizationSwitchToken` and removes the previous organisation listener.
3. A Firestore listener started for company A does not call the callback that now belongs to company B (`lib/firebase/subscribeOrgCollection.ts`).
4. `runOrgLoad` does not keep a successful cache entry when the epoch changed during the request.
5. Booking creates and edits do not write the response into the store when the epoch changed.
6. iOS `SmartCacheService` and `PersistenceService` key memory and UserDefaults by the current organisation id, and by user id. Same-user sign-out must **not** clear `cached_organizationId`. Login restore calls `chooseSessionOrganization` with that key as `rememberedOrganizationId`. Clear the key only when a different uid signs in (`shouldClearRememberedOrganization`).

## Firebase authorisation

`canAccessOrganization` is membership in that organisation (the user document, the members map, or the creator). On the web rules, the platform owner account is also allowed. An admin flag on the user document is not membership in every company. `organizationExists` is not access.

A signed-in user can still list organisation documents. The apps query that list with `members.{uid}` or `creatorUserId`. Tightening the list rule further has to stay compatible with those two queries. Subcollection reads and writes, and a direct read of another company's organisation document, are membership-scoped.

Other role gaps inside one company (for example who may edit settings) are listed in `project-planner-ios/app-tests/rules/firestore.rules.test.mjs`. Closing the cross-company hole does not by itself close those.

## First divergences this architecture closes

Warning counts differed because the scan window was calculated twice, and because qualification, unverified, and unbooked rows were selected twice. Web used the organisation time zone. iOS used `Calendar.current`, so a person outside the UK could be on a different calendar day, and an empty payment-run list fell back to days 1–2 instead of the half-month default. The shared window and the shared row functions are the fix. The count is whatever those functions produce from the same data, settings, and time zone. It is not copied from one screen onto the other.

Bookings booked for a pending invitee were stored under the placeholder id (`users/{UUID}`). Accepting the invite copies the profile to `users/{authUid}` and deletes the placeholder, so those bookings matched nobody: Daily Overview dropped the row and Warnings flagged the person as unbooked on the days they were booked. `formerUserIds` and `labourPersonIds` close that. Separately, the picker and the unbooked rule disagreed on who is labour (role-only admins were flagged but not bookable; users with no role flags were bookable but never flagged); both now use `labourBookingKind`. iOS additionally decoded `operativeId` as a UUID, so a web booking under a Firebase Auth uid never reached iOS Daily Overview or iOS Warnings.

Bookings differed for the same reason when a listener for company A could still publish into the store after a switch to company B, and when local caches were not keyed by organisation.

## File reports

"Report a problem with this file" is `lib/canonical/fileReports.ts`. A report is `organizations/{orgId}/fileReports/{reportId}` (uppercase UUID) with `organizationId`, `storagePath`, `surface`, `parentId`, `fileName`, `reason`, `details`, `reportedByUserId`, `status: 'open'` and a server `createdAt`. `fileReportWriteFields` builds it and refuses a path outside `organizations/{orgId}/`, an unknown `reason` (`FILE_REPORT_REASONS`) or `surface` (`FILE_REPORT_SURFACES`), or a missing parent. `fileReportStoragePath` turns a download URL into the Storage path; the download URL and its token are never stored. Details are capped at `FILE_REPORT_DETAILS_MAX`. Reason and status labels are `fileReportReasonCopy` and `fileReportStatusCopy`. `canReviewFileReports` is admins and super admins; they close a report with `fileReportReviewWriteFields` (`reviewed` or `removed`, plus `reviewedByUserId` and a server `reviewedAt`). Firestore rules let anyone in the organisation create a report as themselves, let only admins and the platform owner read and close it, and never allow delete. The link appears on site audit photos, task attachments, health and safety files, deadline files, variation evidence, and another person's qualification certificates. Profile photos and company logos are not reportable.

## Tax identifiers (UTR and VAT number)

`lib/canonical/sensitiveProfile.ts`. UTR and VAT number live at `users/{uid}/private/taxIds` (`sensitiveProfileDocPath`), fields `utrNumber`, `vatNumber`, `updatedByUserId` and a server `updatedAt`, written through `sensitiveProfileWriteFields` (trimmed, at most `SENSITIVE_VALUE_MAX` characters, blank clears). `canViewSensitiveProfile` is the person themselves, or an admin or super admin not in operative mode; managers and colleagues see `SENSITIVE_HELD_BY_ADMIN_COPY`. Firestore rules match that: the person, an admin of their company and the platform owner read and write; the invitee may read and delete the placeholder's copy while finishing an invite. `maskSensitiveValue` shows the last four characters. Exports leave both out unless `exportIncludesSensitiveFields` (asked for, and the exporter may see them; label `SENSITIVE_EXPORT_OPTION_LABEL`). Until the one-off migration runs, `resolveSensitiveProfile` falls back to `utrNumber`/`vatNumber` still on `users/{uid}`; saves write only the private document and remove the old fields. They are never written to `invitations/{id}` or to `users/{uid}`, and `USER_DOCUMENT_FIELDS` no longer lists them. Analytics metadata drops tax ids, phone numbers and pay rates (`sanitizeEventMetadata`).
